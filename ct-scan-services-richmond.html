MAXIMUM RESOLUTION IMAGING CENTER — CT SCAN SERVICES IN RICHMOND, TX

Maximum Resolution Imaging Center has positioned itself as the leading provider of CT Scan services in Richmond, TX by investing in advanced multi-detector computed tomography technology. The center's CT system produces rapid, high-resolution cross-sectional images that are among the most detailed available in Fort Bend County — supporting a wide range of clinical indications from emergency trauma to complex oncological staging.

CT technology generates detailed images by combining multiple X-Ray measurements taken from different angles — a process that is significantly faster than MRI and exceptionally effective for evaluating bones, blood vessels, organs, and internal soft tissue structures.

💡 Clinical Note: The Radiological Society of North America recognizes CT as one of the most effective tools available for detecting internal injuries, evaluating cancer progression, and guiding interventional medical procedures — valued universally for its speed and anatomical precision.

CT SCAN STUDIES PERFORMED AT THIS RICHMOND, TX FACILITY

Head & Brain CT

Maximum Resolution Imaging Center performs head CT studies for the rapid evaluation of stroke, intracranial hemorrhage, traumatic brain injury, and suspected intracranial mass. Given CT's speed advantage over MRI, this modality is the preferred imaging choice for acute neurological presentations where time-critical diagnosis is essential.

Chest CT

The facility's chest CT protocols evaluate the lungs, heart, great vessels, pleural spaces, and mediastinum — identifying pulmonary embolism, lung nodules, pneumonia, pleural effusion, and thoracic aortic pathology. Both standard and high-resolution chest CT protocols are available depending on the clinical question.

Abdominal & Pelvic CT

Abdominal and pelvic CT is one of the most frequently ordered studies at Maximum Resolution Imaging Center — providing comprehensive evaluation of the liver, spleen, kidneys, pancreas, bowel, and pelvic structures for appendicitis, diverticulitis, renal calculi, and tumor staging.

CT Angiography (CTA)

The center performs CT Angiography studies of the coronary arteries, carotid arteries, aorta, and peripheral vasculature — offering Fort Bend County physicians a minimally invasive vascular imaging alternative to conventional catheter angiography across a broad range of clinical indications.

CT with & without Contrast

Maximum Resolution Imaging Center offers CT imaging with and without intravenous contrast enhancement — improving the detection of vascular abnormalities, active inflammation, and soft tissue tumors throughout the body based on each patient's specific clinical needs.
